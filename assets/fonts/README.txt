Fonts used by this site
=======================

Fraunces            — SIL Open Font License 1.1
                      https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces
Plus Jakarta Sans   — SIL Open Font License 1.1
                      https://github.com/tokotype/PlusJakartaSans

Both are free for commercial use, including self-hosting like this.
The OFL requires that the fonts stay under the same licence and that this
notice is kept — do not delete this file.

They are served from this folder rather than from Google's servers so that:
  1. the site loads faster (one fewer connection to a third party), and
  2. your customers' IP addresses are never sent to Google, which is the
     right default under Malaysia's PDPA 2010.
