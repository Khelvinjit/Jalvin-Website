Terms & conditions

1. What we do

We take your order and payment first, then buy the goods from farmers and market suppliers on your behalf, then deliver them to you. We do not hold stock. This means your order is a request for us to purchase specific goods for you, and it is only fulfilled once those goods have actually been bought.

2. Prices

Prices are set from the market rate at the time of listing and change week to week. The price shown when you place your order is the price you pay — we will never ask you for more after you have paid, even if the market price rises before we shop. All prices are in Malaysian Ringgit and include all charges. Delivery is shown as a separate line before you pay.

3. Pack sizes

Fresh produce is sold in fixed packs at fixed prices. Every listing states the pack size as an approximate, because natural produce cannot be portioned exactly. We aim to stay within roughly 10% of the stated weight. If a pack arrives noticeably short, tell us and we will refund the difference.

4. Order cut-offs

Cut-offs are firm. Once a cut-off has passed, that delivery date is removed from checkout automatically and cannot be reinstated.

5. Payment

Payment is by DuitNow QR only, in full, at the time of ordering. Your order is not confirmed until we have verified your payment and sent you a confirmation on WhatsApp. We will never ask you for your banking password, TAC, OTP or card details, and you should treat anyone who does as a fraudster, including anyone claiming to be us.

6. Cancellation

You may cancel any time before the cut-off for a full refund. After the cut-off we have already purchased your goods with our own funds and cannot cancel them.

7. Our right to decline

We may decline or cancel an order and refund you in full if we cannot verify payment, if the delivery address falls outside our service area, or if we are unable to source enough of what you ordered to make the order worthwhile.

8. Records

We keep records of each transaction for two years, as required by the Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transactions) Regulations 2012.

9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Malaysia. Nothing here limits your statutory rights under the Consumer Protection Act 1999.

Refund & substitution policy

When something is out of stock

Market stock changes by the hour. If we cannot get an item you ordered, we contact you on WhatsApp from the market, before deciding anything. You choose your preference in advance at checkout:

    When you are not happy with the quality

    Tell us on the day of delivery and send a photo. If an item arrived in a condition you would not have bought at the market yourself, we refund that item in full. You do not need to return it — returning fresh produce helps nobody.

    How refunds are paid

    Refunds are made by bank transfer to the account you paid from, within 24 hours of delivery, and we send you the transfer receipt. We do not issue store credit unless you specifically ask for it.

    What is not refundable

    Goods that were delivered correctly and in good condition, but that you changed your mind about after delivery. Fresh food cannot be resold once it has left our cooler, so we cannot take it back.

    Halal & non-halal handling

    Please read this before ordering

    We sell both halal chicken and non-halal pork products. Because of that, this business as a whole is not halal-certified, and we will not describe it as such. What we commit to instead is strict separation, set out below.

    Our chicken

    All chicken we sell is sourced from a JAKIM-certified halal supplier.

    How the two are kept apart

    • Separate sourcing. Halal and non-halal goods are bought from different suppliers and never carried in the same container from the market.
    • Separate storage. Different cooler boxes, different ice packs, different containers. Nothing is shared between the two.
    • Separate packing. Non-halal items are sealed in their own bags and packed separately, using separate gloves and separate surfaces.
    • Separate delivery. If your order contains both, they arrive as two sealed packages that never touch each other.
    • Separate on your order. Your order confirmation lists halal and non-halal items as two distinct sections, so you can check exactly what is in each bag.

    If you would rather not order from a shop that sells pork at all

    We completely understand, and we would rather you knew now than found out later. That is why the non-halal section is clearly marked everywhere on this site and never mixed into general listings.

    Privacy notice

    Issued under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

    What we collect

    Your name, WhatsApp number, delivery address and postcode, your order details, and any notes you choose to send us. If you leave a review, we also collect your first name and the area you live in.

    Why we collect it

    To process your order, verify your payment, buy the right goods, deliver to the right address, contact you if something sells out, and handle refunds. We cannot fulfil an order without this information.

    Who we share it with

    Nobody, other than the person delivering your order. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We do not use third-party advertising trackers on this website.

    Where it is stored

    Your order reaches us as a WhatsApp message and stays in that chat. A copy of your own recent orders is stored in your own web browser so that the tracking and reorder features work — that copy never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

    How long we keep it

    Two years, as required for transaction records under the Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transactions) Regulations 2012, after which it is deleted.

    Your rights

    You may ask us at any time to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Message us on WhatsApp and we will action it. You may also withdraw consent for us to contact you, though we will then be unable to process further orders.

    Reviews

    If you send us a review, we publish it only if it is 5 stars and you have given us permission. We publish only your first name and general area — never your phone number, address or order details. Reviews below 5 stars are never published; they go to the owner so we can fix what went wrong. Ask us to remove a published review and we will.
