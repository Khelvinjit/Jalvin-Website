Verifying a payment

  1. Find the order number in your bank's DuitNow reference.
  2. Check the amount matches the order total exactly.
  3. Set the status below to Payment verified.
  4. Reply on WhatsApp so the customer knows.

Reviews

5-star reviews can be published once you approve them. Anything below 5 stars stays here, for your eyes only.

Add a review you received

Only tick this if they actually said yes. Publishing someone's words without asking is both rude and a PDPA problem.

Why the split

Publishing only 5-star reviews means your public page shows your best reviews, not a representative sample. The website says that out loud on the homepage and the review form, so nobody is misled. Reviews below 5 stars are worth more to you than any of the good ones — they are the only place your next improvement is written down.

SettingNowWhere to change it
Before you go live, replace the placeholders. Malaysian law requires an online shop to show its registered business name, SSM number, email and phone. Those are in the business block of config.js, and they appear in the footer of every page. Also replace assets/img/duitnow-qr.svg with your own DuitNow QR, and change the admin passcode.
