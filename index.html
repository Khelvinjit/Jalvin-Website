How it works

Four steps, and we WhatsApp you at every one

The only unusual part is that you pay before we shop. Here is exactly what happens to your money in between.

You order & pay

Fill your basket, pick Wednesday or Saturday, and pay by DuitNow QR. You get an order number — type it into the DuitNow reference box so we can match your payment instantly.

We verify

We check your payment against your order and WhatsApp you a confirmation, usually within two hours. Nothing is bought until this is done.

We buy it fresh

Early on delivery morning we go to the farm and the market with your list in hand. If something has sold out we message you from the stall before deciding anything.

We deliver

Straight from the market into a cooler box and to your door in Cyberjaya, in the time window you chose. Fish and chicken stay chilled the whole way.

Order cut-offs. Wednesday delivery closes Monday 11:59 PM. Saturday delivery closes Thursday 11:59 PM. These are firm — after the cut-off there is no time left to buy your goods before the delivery run.
